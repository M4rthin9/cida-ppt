# Vocational design system

The public experience is a modern craft gallery with warm white surfaces, burgundy accents, generous whitespace and Thai-first typography. The public palette uses ink `#282326`, secondary text `#6e6569`, paper `#faf9f6`, burgundy `#781c3b` and charcoal `#30262c`. It describes professional practice and renewed opportunity without security imagery or fictional institutional claims.

- Keep the exact institutional emblem and its proportions. Never redraw Thai lettering.
- The existing locally hosted Anuphan family supports Thai marks. Use comfortable line-height and no artificial letter-spacing on Thai text.
- The static introduction pairs clear Thai typography with the existing conceptual artwork in a bounded image panel. The image remains explicitly labeled as an illustration, never product photography. Cinematic hero activation remains deferred; the separate player and frame preparation guide are not connected to the homepage.
- Use burgundy for primary actions and current navigation. Quiet borders, 16–24px surface corners, pill-shaped public actions and restrained hover transitions unify the public pages. Colored category surfaces remain typographic until authentic images are supplied.
- Category bento cards are database-driven. Uploaded category images replace typographic cover treatments; no generated product images are substituted.
- Product cards contain an authentic image or clear no-image state, category, name, price mode and detail link. No checkout, discount-marketplace badges or invented stock.
- The CMS uses white panels on a cloud canvas with the same burgundy identity: clear sidebar, useful counts, tables, searchable filters, sectioned forms, inline errors, confirmations and persistent data. The login pairs an institutional introduction with a focused sign-in form, stacking compactly on mobile.
- Body copy and form controls use 16px as their baseline; labels use 14px where practical. Preserve field error colors, visible keyboard focus and sufficient line-height for Thai marks. English supporting labels may use tracking; Thai labels do not.
- Mobile uses stacked cards and forms, a collapsible public menu and tables that scroll within their own containers.
- Use semantic controls, keyboard focus, labels, alt text and reduced-motion support. Preserve product images with proportional rendering and stored originals.

Public styles: `src/app/vocational.css`. CMS styles: `src/app/cms.css`. Existing design tokens remain available to retained authentication, settings and contact components.

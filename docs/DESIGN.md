# Vocational design system

The public experience is cinematic, editorial and focused on craftsmanship. Cream paper surfaces, deep charcoal/olive, muted burgundy and generous whitespace support Thai-first typography. It describes professional practice and renewed opportunity without security imagery or fictional institutional claims.

- Keep the exact institutional emblem and its proportions. Never redraw Thai lettering.
- The existing locally hosted Anuphan family supports Thai marks. Use comfortable line-height and no artificial letter-spacing on Thai text.
- The hero uses the supplied 150-frame sequence when available, driven solely by scroll. The fallback artwork is labeled as a conceptual illustration and is never passed off as product photography.
- Category bento cards are database-driven. Uploaded category images replace typographic cover treatments; no generated product images are substituted.
- Product cards contain an authentic image or clear no-image state, category, name, price mode and detail link. No checkout, discount-marketplace badges or invented stock.
- The CMS is visually separate: clear sidebar, useful counts, tables, searchable filters, sectioned forms, inline errors, confirmations and persistent data.
- Mobile uses stacked cards and forms, a collapsible public menu and tables that scroll within their own containers.
- Use semantic controls, keyboard focus, labels, alt text and reduced-motion support. Preserve product images with proportional rendering and stored originals.

Public styles: `src/app/vocational.css`. CMS styles: `src/app/cms.css`. Existing design tokens remain available to retained authentication, settings and contact components.
